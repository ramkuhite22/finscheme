# Indian Central Government Schemes (2025–2026 Context)

This context document has been generated to enrich the FinScheme discovery map, eligibility wizard, and jargon humanizer database with the latest national schemes launched in the 2025–2026 fiscal cycle.

## 1. Agricultural & Rural Schemes

### **Bharat-VISTAAR**
- **Overview**: A multilingual AI-based platform designed to integrate agricultural resources and provide customized advisory support directly to farmers.
- **Target Audience**: Farmers, agricultural workers, and rural communities.
- **Finscheme Integration**: Ideal for inclusion in the "Guided Discovery Wizard" under the primary agricultural filters.

### **Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana**
- **Overview**: Focused on enhancing agricultural productivity, improving soil health, and ensuring rural economic prosperity.
- **Target Audience**: Small and marginal landowners.

### **National Mission on High-Yielding Seeds**
- **Overview**: Special program to scale up seed production of high-yielding, pest-resistant crop varieties across the country.

## 2. Business & Entrepreneurship Schemes

### **Credit Cards for Micro Enterprises**
- **Overview**: Provides customized credit cards with a credit limit of up to ₹5 lakh for micro-enterprises registered on the Udyam portal.
- **Target Audience**: Micro-enterprises and small vendors.
- **Finscheme Integration**: Should be tagged under the financial/business categories in the relationship map.

### **Fund of Funds for Startups (Expansion)**
- **Overview**: Expansion of startup funding support with a fresh contribution of ₹10,000 crore to encourage innovations and job creation.

---

## 3. Education & Skill Development Schemes

### **Creative Economy Support (AVGC Schools & Colleges)**
- **Overview**: Establishment of specialized Visual Effects, Gaming, and Comics (AVGC) Content Creator Labs in 15,000 secondary schools and 500 colleges.
- **Target Audience**: Students, educational institutions, and young creators.

### **Hospitality Training Initiative**
- **Overview**: Upskilling and standardized certification of 10,000 tourist guides across 20 major historical sites.
- **Target Audience**: Unemployed youths, local tour guides.

Big 6 documents

Keep these ready first

  • Aadhaar linked to a mobile number for OTPs
  • Maharashtra domicile for state-specific benefits
  • Fresh income certificate from the Tahsildar
  • 10th or 12th marksheets plus current bonafide
  • Caste certificate and validity where applicable
  • Bank passbook with Aadhaar-seeded NPCI mapping
Top rejection traps

What usually goes wrong

  • Name mismatch between Aadhaar and bank records
  • Expired income certificate from the previous cycle
  • Live-photo mismatch during final submission
  • Large or blurry PDF uploads that fail portal checks
Status terms that matter
Under scrutiny

Your documents are being checked by the reviewing office.

Defect rectification

An error was found and you usually need to fix it within the portal deadline.

Approved or disbursed

The money is cleared and should move to the linked bank account.

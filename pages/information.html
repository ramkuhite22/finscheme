Master Document Checklist

The "Big 6" documents commonly required across schemes.

Identity

Aadhaar card (mobile linked for OTP).

Residence

Maharashtra domicile certificate for state benefits.

Income

Income certificate issued by Tahsildar (typically valid for 1 year).

Education

10th/12th marksheets, TC/leaving certificate, current bonafide.

Caste

Caste certificate and validity for SC/ST/OBC/VJNT.

Banking

Passbook with Aadhaar-NPCI seeding for DBT.

Every Indian citizen should be aware of these 75+ critical laws. We've compiled the most important rights from civil, criminal, labor, insurance, government employment, and professional career law to keep you empowered.

Career & Economic Empowerment

41

Agency Rights (LIC)

Any Indian citizen (18+, 10th pass) can become an LIC agent. Enjoy unlimited earning potential, flexible hours, and professional autonomy.

Self-Employment
42

Hereditary Commission

Unique Right: Renewal commissions for LIC agents can continue to be paid to their legal heirs, ensuring long-term family financial security.

Family Security
43

Agency Perks & Advances

Qualified agents are entitled to interest-free advances for cars, office setup, and laptops, plus group insurance and gratuity benefits.

Insurance & Financial Rights

31

The 'Free Look' Period

You have 15 days (30 if digital) to cancel ANY life insurance policy after receiving the bond. You are entitled to a refund of your premium.

Government Employment Schemes

51

Mukhyamantri Yojana Doot

Maharashtra's scheme to appoint 50,000 Yojana Doots statewide to promote government welfare programmes at the grassroots level.

₹10,000/month
52

Eligibility Rights

Open to Maharashtra residents aged 18–35, with a minimum graduation degree, smartphone, basic computer knowledge, and an Aadhaar-linked bank account.

18–35 Years
53

How to Apply

Apply online at rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in. Select 'Employment' → choose 'Yojana Doot Bharti' and upload required documents including Aadhaar, degree certificate, and photograph.

100% Online
54

Know Your Contract Rights

The appointment is for a fixed 6-month term with no extension. It includes travel allowance, but does NOT lead to a permanent government job.

6-Month Contract

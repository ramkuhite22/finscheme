Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us make the site function properly, make it more secure, provide better user experience, and understand how the website performs.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies for user authentication, maintaining active sessions, improving site performance through analytics tracking, and remembering your preferences (like your language settings).

3. Controlling Cookies

If you prefer not to use cookies, you can change your browser settings to delete or block them. However, doing so might limit certain features and overall usability of the FinScheme platform.

4. More Information

For more details on how we handle personal data, please review our Privacy Policy. Have questions? Reach out to privacy@finscheme.com.

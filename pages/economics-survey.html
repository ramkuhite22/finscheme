What this collects

We focus on the real household impact of schemes: awareness, participation, expense relief, and financial behavior.

  • Basic respondent details like name, age group, state, and livelihood.
  • Whether schemes reduce monthly pressure on education, bills, and credit dependence.
  • Submission metadata for Google Sheets and analytics reporting.
Responses from this page go through a dedicated submit endpoint, so you are no longer dependent on a hosted Google Form to collect survey data.
